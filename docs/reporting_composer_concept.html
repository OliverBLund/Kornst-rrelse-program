What changes in the real UI

Rail

The current sidebar is replaced by a compact step rail that does not try to hold every control at once.

Samples

Many samples are handled in a table-like browser with selection states and a compact tray for what is currently active.

Structure

Appendix, metadata, and branding become separate steps instead of one continuous scroll stack.

Design thesis

Move the reporting tab from a deep sidebar into a composer: narrow rail, focused workbench, live inspector. The user should never have to scroll past branding and notes to manage samples.

Why this should work better

Scales with many samples

A table browser handles density better than a checkbox stack and supports grouping, status scanning, and selection at a glance.

Less sidebar overload

Only the current step is foregrounded. The rest collapses into the rail and inspector.

Clearer reporting flow

Compose scope, shape appendix, then export. The order matches how people actually decide what the report is.

Next port

If this direction lands, the first production step should be moving sample selection into a browser-like component and collapsing the rest of the sidebar into step sections.
