Methods Overview

The program includes a broad method set. Use this page to understand the method groups and where to look for applicability, not to replace the status checks shown in the Results tab.

Do not read methods in isolation

Always check the Status column and the method detail panel in the dataset Results tab. A method can be present in the app and still be a poor fit for the current sample.

Method Set In The App

Current calculations cover Hazen, Hazen 1892, Slichter, Terzaghi, Beyer, Sauerbrei, Kruger, Kozeny-Carman, Zunker, Zamarin, USBR, Barr, Alyamani-Sen, Chapuis, Shepherd, and Krumbein-Monk.

Typical Inputs

Type of input Common examples
Single characteristic diameter D10, D17, D20, or D50 depending on method
Gradation terms Cu, Cc, or other curve-shape measures
Porosity-sensitive terms Kozeny-Carman and other methods that depend strongly on porosity assumptions
Whole-curve measures Methods that derive effective diameters from the full grain-size distribution

Operational Rule

If you are deciding what to report, start with methods that stay inside their recommended range for the current sample and treat large disagreement between methods as something to explain, not something to hide.
